For Figma Make, you'll get much better results if you **don't ask for a website**.

Ask for a **cinematic interactive experience**.

Most people prompt:

> "Create a research vault website with dark theme."

And Figma generates another SaaS dashboard.

You want something much more ambitious.

---

# MASTER FIGMA MAKE PROMPT

Design a premium futuristic web experience called **"The Research Vault"**.

This is not a traditional website, portfolio, blog, dashboard, SaaS application, or documentation platform.

The experience should feel like entering a legendary archive of human knowledge located outside space and time.

The visual identity combines:

* Library of Alexandria
* Interstellar
* Dune
* NASA Mission Control
* Ancient observatories
* Obsidian knowledge graph
* High-end museum installations
* Cinematic science-fiction interfaces

The mood should feel:

* Grand
* Intelligent
* Timeless
* Mysterious
* Historic
* Futuristic
* Exploratory
* Scholarly

Avoid:

* Startup SaaS aesthetics
* Generic dashboards
* Corporate UI
* Neon cyberpunk overload
* Simple card grids
* Generic portfolio layouts

Use a dark luxury theme with:

* Deep space backgrounds
* Stone and archive-inspired textures
* Subtle gold accents
* Glassmorphism only where appropriate
* Holographic information panels
* Depth and layered environments
* Large-scale architecture
* Cinematic lighting

The website should feel like a physical place that can be explored.

---

### LANDING EXPERIENCE

Create a dramatic landing sequence.

The user arrives before a massive ancient-futuristic vault door floating in a cosmic void.

Behind the door are stars, constellations, and floating knowledge structures.

Large title:

THE RESEARCH VAULT

Subtitle:

A lifelong archive of curiosity, exploration, and learning.

Primary interaction:

Enter The Vault

The entrance should feel monumental and immersive.

---

### HALL OF DOMAINS

After entering, reveal a giant knowledge hall.

The hall contains multiple knowledge domains represented as architectural wings, portals, or floating structures.

Domains include:

Technology

Science

Humanities

Literature

Psychology

History

Philosophy

Business

Future Studies

Space Exploration

Each domain should feel unique while belonging to the same world.

Navigation should feel like exploration rather than clicking menu items.

---

### KNOWLEDGE GALAXY

Create a massive interactive knowledge map.

Represent topics as stars, planets, nodes, or glowing artifacts.

Connections between topics form visible constellations.

Examples:

Psychology connected to Neuroscience, Philosophy, Behavioral Economics, and Education.

Artificial Intelligence connected to Robotics, Data Science, Ethics, and Future Studies.

Users can zoom, pan, and explore.

The visualization should resemble a living universe of knowledge.

This is one of the main signature experiences.

---

The layout should feel like entering a research room rather than reading a blog page.

Include immersive visual storytelling.

---

### DESIGN QUALITY

The final result should feel like:

A museum exhibit created by NASA, the Library of Alexandria, and the Interstellar production team.

Prioritize atmosphere, storytelling, exploration, and wonder over traditional website conventions.

The experience should feel like entering a living universe of knowledge rather than browsing pages.

---

One more tip before you burn through 3000 credits:

Generate **each major area separately** (Landing Hall, Knowledge Galaxy, Topic Chamber, Library, Nexus, Observatory) rather than one giant prompt. Figma Make often produces better results when each screen is treated as a standalone masterpiece, then stitched together into a unified design system.
